SFSP: the rules live inside the money.

SFSP, the Social Financial System Protocol, is the asset protocol of the Social Financial System. In an ordinary token, compliance lives outside, in an app or a paper contract. In SFSP the asset is born knowing who issues it, what backs it, who may hold it and how much risk it carries. No contract that fails to comply will run on the 5550 chain.

How it works

Every asset is born with its passport
Before anyone can buy it, the chain records who issues it, what backs it, who audited and valued it, and its risk level, from R1 to R5. Every change keeps the previous version in view.
The rule lives inside the asset
Who may hold it and who it may go to is not a warning in a document: if a transfer does not comply, the chain rejects it.
One identity, without exposing your data
Genesis ID checks who you are and the chain keeps only the proof. Your documents and balances stay out of the public record.
Everything leaves a trace
Every issuance, burn or change of status emits a public event with its reason. What leaves no event did not happen.

One system, three pillars

Orden Global
Runs the network, issues and executes what is authorised. It runs no market where its own currencies trade.
DBNX
Admits, classifies and supervises whoever wants to issue. It neither issues nor values.
Au Corp.
Runs the system's market, Ordenex. It does not issue.

No single key can create money, touch the reserves or move someone else's balance: anything that matters needs several signatures, a written reason and a waiting period.
