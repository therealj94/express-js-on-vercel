SFSP: the rules live inside the money

SFSP, the Social Financial System Protocol, is the asset protocol of the Social Financial System. In an ordinary token, the rules live outside, in an app or a paper contract. In SFSP the asset is born knowing who issues it, what backs it, who may hold it and how much risk it carries. No contract that fails to comply will run on the 5550.

Orden Global

Runs the network, issues and executes what is authorised. It runs no market where its own currencies trade.

DBNX

Admits, classifies and supervises whoever wants to issue. It neither issues nor values.

Au Corp.

Runs the system's market, Ordenex. It does not issue.

Three pillars with separate roles, so that whoever issues is never the one who sets the price.
