SFSP: the rules, written before they are switched on.

Under construction · nothing live on the 5550

SFSP, the Social Financial System Protocol, will set the rules for everything issued, exchanged and settled on the 5550 chain. We are writing and testing it before switching it on, so that when it runs, exactly what it does is already known.
