ORIGEN, the currency

It rises and falls with gold. We don't set it.
1 ORIGEN = 1 gram of gold ÷ 55

The formula is public. With today's price of a gram of gold and a calculator, anyone reaches the same number they see in their wallet.

Said plainly: ORIGEN follows the price of gold. It is not a receipt for stored metal: it is a reference anyone can recompute. We promise no future prices and no returns.
